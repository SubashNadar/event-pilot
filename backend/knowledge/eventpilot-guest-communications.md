# EventPilot Guest Communication Playbook

Send the first invitation with event date, location, RSVP link, and response deadline. Follow with a reminder one to two weeks before the event and a final event-day arrival note containing parking, transport, and venue contact information.

For a wedding reception, confirm family contact names, arrival timing, photography moments, dining flow, and elder-access requirements. Avoid publishing a detailed schedule until the family coordinator has approved it.

For a workshop, share the agenda, venue access, required materials, and arrival time in advance. Send a short preparation note no later than three working days before the session.
