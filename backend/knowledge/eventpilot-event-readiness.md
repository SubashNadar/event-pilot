# EventPilot Event Readiness Guide

For an event with more than 150 guests, confirm a registration or welcome zone separate from the main event space. The venue should have an accessible entrance, clear parking or transport guidance, and a nominated venue contact for event day.

Collect dietary requirements during RSVP. Confirm the final catering count at least three days before the event and keep a five percent contingency for late confirmations. Clearly label vegetarian and allergen-aware menu options.

For outdoor or partially outdoor events, record an indoor or covered fallback before confirming the venue. For programmes that rely on AV, request a venue power-backup plan and complete an AV check before guests arrive.

An EventPilot plan is ready for approval only after every listed risk has a recorded action. Exporting a final blueprint is available after approval; a preview can be reviewed beforehand.
